Dining

Food here follows the pace of the trip: bright plates after lake walks, slow pasta at dusk, and small courses worth lingering over. Dining notes are woven into every Travel package.

  • Dinner

    Broth & mountain herb tortellini

    Small pasta parcels in a warm citrus broth, finished with herbs and a little brine for contrast.

  • Lunch

    Green pasta with soft burrata

    A bright midday plate with silky greens, olive oil, and cool cheese at the center.

  • Supper

    Ravioli in alpine cream

    Soft green ravioli, a light cream sauce, and enough richness to feel like the day has landed.

  • Small plate

    Herb dumplings with red pepper sauce

    A sharper, brighter plate built for sharing before the larger courses arrive.

  • Coastal night

    Seafood pasta with tomato and herbs

    A coastal counterpoint to the mountain menu, with tomato, herbs, and clean seafood flavor.

  • For two

    Cream sauce pasta duo

    Two quiet bowls for an unhurried evening: one lighter, one richer, both made for passing across the table.
